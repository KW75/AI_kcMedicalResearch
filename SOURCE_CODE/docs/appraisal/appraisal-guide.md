# Critical Appraisal Guide

## Purpose
This guide is injected into every appraisal session.
The Appraiser, Methodologist, and Summariser roles must follow it exactly.

## Mandatory 7-Section Report Structure
Every appraisal report must contain these sections in this order.
Total word limit: 1500 words across all seven sections.

### 1. Study Details
- Full citation (authors, title, journal, year, DOI)
- Study design (RCT, cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, systematic review, etc.)
- Country and setting
- Funding source and declared conflicts of interest

### 2. Research Question / PICO
- **P** Population: who was studied?
- **I** Intervention / exposure: what was done or measured?
- **C** Comparison: control group or reference?
- **O** Outcome(s): primary and secondary endpoints
- Was the research question clearly stated in the paper? (Yes / No / Partial)

### 3. Methodology
- Sample size and power calculation (adequate / inadequate / not reported)
- Recruitment and eligibility criteria
- Randomisation and allocation concealment (for RCTs)
- Blinding (participants, assessors — for RCTs)
- Follow-up duration and completeness
- Statistical methods appropriate for outcome type? (Yes / No)

### 4. Results Summary
- Primary outcome: effect size, confidence interval, p-value
- Secondary outcomes: brief summary
- Number needed to treat / harm (NNT / NNH) if applicable
- Any subgroup analyses — were they pre-specified?

### 5. Risk of Bias
Apply the appropriate checklist (see scoring-criteria.md).
Rate overall bias: **Low / Moderate / High / Critical**
Summarise the key bias domains that drove the rating.

### 6. Clinical Applicability
- Does the population match your clinical context? (Yes / Partial / No)
- Is the intervention feasible in your setting?
- Do the benefits outweigh harms at the observed effect size?
- Any important safety signals?

### 7. Overall Judgement
- Quality of evidence: High / Moderate / Low / Very Low (GRADE language)
- Should this evidence change practice? (Yes / No / Uncertain — with brief justification)
- Suggested next step: (e.g. seek confirmatory trials, update guideline, no action needed)

## Word Limits per Section (guideline)
| Section | Suggested limit |
|---------|----------------|
| Study Details | 100 words |
| Research Question / PICO | 150 words |
| Methodology | 300 words |
| Results Summary | 300 words |
| Risk of Bias | 200 words |
| Clinical Applicability | 250 words |
| Overall Judgement | 200 words |
| **Total** | **≤ 1500 words** |

## Plain-Language Summary (Summariser role)
After the 7-section report, the Summariser must append a separate section:

### Plain-Language Summary
- Target: ≤ 200 words
- Audience: patient or non-specialist clinician
- Avoid jargon; if a technical term is unavoidable, define it in parentheses
- State clearly: what was studied, what was found, and what it means for patients

## Study-Type Notes
- **RCT**: focus on randomisation quality, blinding, ITT analysis
- **Cohort / Case-control**: focus on confounding, selection bias, recall bias
- **Systematic review / meta-analysis**: focus on search completeness, heterogeneity (I²), publication bias
- **Cross-sectional**: note inability to establish causality

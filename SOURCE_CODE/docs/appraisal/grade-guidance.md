# GRADE Guidance

## Starting Point
- RCTs: start at HIGH
- Observational studies: start at LOW
- Expert opinion / case series: start at VERY LOW

## Downgrade Criteria (−1 or −2 per domain)
| Domain | −1 (serious) | −2 (very serious) |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Risk of bias | Some concerns in most studies | High risk in most studies |
| Inconsistency | I² 40–60%, unexplained | I² > 60%, conflicting directions |
| Indirectness | Population, intervention or outcome differs somewhat | Major differences from question |
| Imprecision | Wide CI, optimal information size not met | Very wide CI, few events |
| Publication bias | Funnel plot asymmetry suspected | Strong evidence of publication bias |

## Upgrade Criteria (+1 per domain, observational only)
| Domain | Condition |
|--------|-----------|
| Large effect | RR > 2 or < 0.5 with no major confounding |
| Dose-response | Clear gradient across exposure levels |
| Plausible confounding | All confounders would reduce the effect |

## Final Ratings
| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| HIGH | Very confident the true effect lies close to the estimate |
| MODERATE | Moderately confident; true effect likely close but may differ |
| LOW | Limited confidence; true effect may differ substantially |
| VERY LOW | Very little confidence; true effect likely different from estimate |

## Reporting Format
State: **GRADE: [LEVEL]** followed by a one-sentence justification listing
the domains that drove the rating, e.g.:
> GRADE: LOW — downgraded twice for serious risk of bias (open-label design)
> and serious imprecision (wide 95% CI crossing no effect).

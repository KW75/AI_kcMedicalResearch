# Risk of Bias Scoring Criteria

## For Randomised Controlled Trials — RoB 2 (Cochrane)

| Domain | Low risk | Some concerns | High risk |
|--------|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Randomisation process | Truly random, allocation concealed | Method unclear | Not random or unconcealed |
| Deviations from intervention | No important deviations, or ITT analysis used | Some deviations, per-protocol used | Major deviations, no sensitivity analysis |
| Missing outcome data | < 10% missing, reasons balanced | 10–20% missing | > 20% missing or reasons imbalanced |
| Measurement of outcome | Blinded assessors, validated instrument | Assessors aware of allocation | Outcome influenced by knowledge of allocation |
| Selection of reported result | Protocol registered, all outcomes reported | Minor discrepancy from protocol | Selective reporting of outcomes or subgroups |

**Overall RoB 2 rating:** Low (all domains low) / Some concerns (any domain some concerns) / High (any domain high or multiple some-concerns)

| Item | Adequate | Partial | Inadequate |
|------|----------|---------|------------|

**Overall AMSTAR 2 confidence:** High / Moderate / Low / Critically Low

## GRADE Evidence Quality
| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| High | Further research very unlikely to change confidence in effect estimate |
| Moderate | Further research likely to have important impact on confidence |
| Low | Further research very likely to have important impact |
| Very Low | Very uncertain about the effect estimate |

Start at High for RCTs, Low for observational studies.
Downgrade for: risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias.
Upgrade for: large effect, dose-response, all plausible confounders would reduce effect.

# Editorial Standards — Topic Writing

## Accuracy
- All factual claims supported by at least one credible source.
- Statistics reproduced accurately; do not round without noting it.
- Do not misrepresent study findings to strengthen an argument.
- If a claim cannot be verified, flag it with [UNVERIFIED].

## Fairness and Balance
- Acknowledge the strongest counterargument before dismissing it.
- Do not strawman opposing views.
- Distinguish clearly between fact, expert opinion, and the writer's opinion.

## Clarity
- Every paragraph must have a clear topic sentence.
- No sentence should require re-reading to understand.
- Define any retained technical term on first use.

## Originality
- Do not reproduce more than 50 consecutive words from any single source.
- Paraphrase and attribute; do not present others' ideas as original analysis.

## Ethics
- Disclose AI involvement at the foot of any public-facing document.
- Do not fabricate quotes or attribute views to named individuals without citation.
- Avoid stigmatising language around health conditions, populations, or behaviours.

## Structure Requirements
- Opening hook present.
- Thesis stated in the first paragraph.
- Conclusion restates key message and ends decisively.
- Word count within agreed limit.

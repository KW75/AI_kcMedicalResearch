# Supported Article Types

## Fixed Article Types
The following article types are supported in Article Search mode.
Use the exact label when filtering PubMed results.

| Label | PubMed Filter | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Systematic Review | systematic[sb] | Comprehensive literature synthesis |
| Meta-analysis | meta-analysis[pt] | Statistical pooling of study results |
| RCT | randomized controlled trial[pt] | Randomised controlled trial |
| Guideline | guideline[pt] | Clinical practice guideline |
| Protocol | research support[pt] | Study protocol or research protocol |
| Review Article | review[pt] | Narrative or scoping review |
| Clinical Trial | clinical trial[pt] | Any registered clinical trial |

## Custom Types
Users may enter any free-text article type at runtime.
Custom types are passed as PubMed title/abstract keyword filters.
